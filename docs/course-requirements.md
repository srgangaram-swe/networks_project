12 to visuals, 15 to clarity and teamwork, 10 to engagement and 3 to resources
and preparation methods. Optional creativity credit is at the instructor's
discretion. A walkthrough of the software alone does not meet the insight goal.

The report assigns 10 points to introduction, 15 to related work, 15 to
technical implementation, 8 to cost, 8 to community, 20 to findings, 6 to
conclusion, 8 to the required reflection and 10 to writing and citations.
The current draft is not a claim that every grading criterion is complete.
