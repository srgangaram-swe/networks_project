Conclusions

Read latency, goodput, delivery outcomes, and uncertainty together. A low on-time p99 alone does not establish a successful treatment.
K-means and agglomerative clustering use two groups. DBSCAN can mark noise or find a different number of groups. No run is excluded automatically.
PCA and t-SNE are views of the fitted features, not additional clustering algorithms. Internal cluster scores measure geometry, not prediction accuracy.
Ridge and Lasso use only design features. Whole blocks are held out, with scaling and alpha selection fitted inside the training folds. This evaluates interpolation among the observed designs.
Finish device setup, timing and calibration checks, protocol review, and primary collection before drawing conclusions about the physical experiment.
More simulated rows exercise the pipeline; they do not strengthen evidence about physical queue policies.
